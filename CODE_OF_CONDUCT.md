# Community code of conduct

AI Lean Muscle Club exists to help AI researchers support one another in making exercise, recovery, and a sustainable daily rhythm possible. We welcome different fitness levels, bodies, schedules, sports, locations, and research backgrounds.

## How we participate

- Be kind and specific. Encourage a person's next manageable step, and ask before giving detailed personal advice.
- Respect different starting points. No one needs to prove their physique, strength, training volume, publication count, or citations to deserve encouragement.
- Make participation voluntary. Members may chat, share, log privately elsewhere, opt out of standings, adjust a plan, or take a break.
- Treat recovery and honest reflection with respect. Missed sessions and incomplete weeks are not a reason for ridicule or pressure.
- Distinguish experience, inference, and evidence. State uncertainty in health advice and Agent-generated suggestions.
- Respect privacy. Share only your own information or material you have permission to publish.

## What does not belong here

Harassment, discrimination, unwanted sexual remarks, body shaming, pressure to exercise through pain or illness, and ridicule of someone's food choices or research status are unacceptable. So are spam, unsolicited commercial advertising, deceptive promotion, fabricated records, and repeated attempts to game the participation system.

Do not use the group to diagnose another member, promise health outcomes, or insist that an Agent's assessment is authoritative. Do not pressure anyone to share photographs, measurements, diagnoses, or identifiable personal information. Keep academic disagreement respectful and relevant to the community's purpose.

## Reporting and moderation

For a routine issue with public content, open a brief issue titled **Moderation request** and link to the content. Avoid repeating offensive or sensitive details unnecessarily. For a sensitive report, use a private contact method listed by a repository maintainer on their GitHub profile instead of posting details publicly. We do not currently operate a dedicated confidential reporting service.

Maintainers may ask for an edit, remove inappropriate content, issue a warning, limit participation, or block an account, depending on the behavior and its impact. They should act fairly, avoid public disclosure of private reports, and explain routine moderation decisions when doing so is appropriate. These expectations apply to maintainers too.

Participation points do not buy special treatment. Our shared priority is an environment where people can ask questions, share an imperfect week, and return after a break.
