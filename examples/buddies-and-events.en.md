# Buddies and events

Post in Discussions → **Buddies and events**. Express interest in the thread first, then let the participants confirm the arrangements.

## Example 1: Starting to exercise together online

**Title:** [Buddies] Looking for a small group without mandatory daily check-ins

**Activity or invitation**
Fictional member Lin would like to find two or three people to choose one day each week, exercise independently, and leave a short record here afterward.

**General availability**
Asia/Shanghai. Weekday evenings are more convenient; the group can discuss the specific day in replies. Everyone chooses their own activity.

**How to join**
Reply with the activity you would like to try and your general availability. You can cancel if something comes up that day; there are no tasks to make up or points to lose.

## Example 2: A basketball activity

**Title:** [Buddies] Thinking of a friendly weekend practice—seeing who is interested first

**Activity or invitation**
Fictional member Zhou would like to arrange an activity focused on conversation and practice. Beginners can express interest too.

**General availability**
A weekend afternoon. Interested members can first discuss a general area and time. This example has no real location or booking.

**How to join**
Reply in the thread if interested. Once participants are confirmed, decide together on a venue, how to split costs, and arrangements for cancellation because of weather. Do not publish participant lists or photographs without their permission.
