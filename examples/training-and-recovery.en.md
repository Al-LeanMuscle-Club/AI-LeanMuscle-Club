# Training conversations and records / Training and recovery

Use the [training and recovery Issue form](../docs/legacy-issue-templates/training-and-recovery.yml) for conversations, with the `[Training]` title prefix. Community conversation does not automatically earn points. For a casual check-in, use an [Activity record Issue](../docs/legacy-issue-templates/activity-record.yml); for a scored record, comment on your own weekly plan.

## Example 1: Recording without a plan

**Title:** [Activity] 2026-10-27 · A walk between experiments

```text
### Activity date
2026-10-27

### Time zone
Asia/Shanghai

### Sport
walking

### What I did
Once the experiment was running, I went outside for a short walk. I did not track the duration, and I felt more ready to get back to the task afterward.

### Optional details
Next time, I would like to invite a classmate to walk and chat.
```

This is a standalone activity record. It does not enter the weekly standings, and exercise metrics are not required.

## Example 2: Completing only part of a planned session

**Community Issue title:** [Training] How do you record a session when your schedule changes unexpectedly?

**Question or experience**
I planned to practice basketball on Tuesday, but something came up and I completed only part of the session. I want to record that honestly and think about how to make future plans easier to follow.

**Context and what I tried**
I left a separate comment on my existing weekly plan:

```text
/partial S1 2026-10-27 | I did part of the practice and did not complete the rest.
Next week, I will first write a workable busy-week alternative for this session.
```

The updated rules give a partial record half a unit of execution credit, while keeping it separate from a full completion. Only confirmed scored sessions enter the calculation. Comment text is for personal records; see the [scoring rules](../docs/SCORING.v2.md) for details.
