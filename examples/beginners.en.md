# New member introductions / Beginners

Post using the [newcomer introduction Issue form](https://github.com/Al-LeanMuscle-Club/AI-LeanMuscle-Club/issues/new?template=beginners.yml) with the `[Hello]` title prefix. You do not need to create a weekly plan or join the standings first. These community Issues do not automatically earn points.

## Example 1: Starting to keep records

**Title:** [Hello] I would like to start with a walk after my experiments

**A short introduction**
Hi everyone, I am Lin, a fictional example member. I usually work on model training. I used to exercise occasionally, and this time I would like a simple way to keep records.

**What I would like to try**
I will start by recording walks and weekend basketball, without setting a task that I must complete every day.

**How members can help**
If you are also trying to fit exercise into breaks between experiments, I would be glad to hear what works for you.
