# Meal records and sharing / Nutrition

Use the [nutrition-sharing Issue form](../docs/legacy-issue-templates/nutrition.yml) for conversations, with the `[Food]` title prefix, and a [Meal record Issue](../docs/legacy-issue-templates/meal-record.yml) for a record. If you already have a weekly plan, you can also post a `/meal` comment on that plan’s Issue. Community sharing and standalone meal records do not automatically earn points.

## Example 1: Lunch without calorie numbers

**Title:** [Meal] 2026-10-28 · Bringing lunch during a busy day of experiments

```text
### Meal date
2026-10-28

### Time zone
Asia/Shanghai

### Food and context
Today I brought rice, tofu, and vegetables that I prepared last night. I did not weigh the food or estimate calories.
What I want to record is that setting aside a portion for lunch in advance meant I did not have to find something to eat at the last minute today.
```

Photos and portion details are optional. This standalone Issue does not earn points or automatically count toward the meal days in a weekly plan.

## Example 2: Recording two meals on the same day

Post two separate comments on your own weekly plan:

```text
/meal 2026-10-29 | Lunch
I bought a meal at the cafeteria. I do not know how much oil was used, so I left the calories blank.
```

```text
/meal 2026-10-29 | Dinner
I made noodles and want to record how I made time to cook today.
```

Both valid dates are the same, so the weekly report shows **1 meal record day**, and the total score stays the same. Eating less, polished photos, or frequent posting should not become competition goals.

**Question for a community Issue:** [Food] How do you prepare tomorrow's lunch when you are busy?
You can share arrangements that fit your own schedule. A nutrition rating or a common meal plan is not required.
